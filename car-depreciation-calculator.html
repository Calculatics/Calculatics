How the Car Depreciation Calculator works

This calculator estimates the future value of a vehicle using a fixed annual depreciation rate.

Each year, the vehicle loses the selected percentage of its value at the start of that year. This means depreciation is calculated on a reducing balance, rather than subtracting the same dollar amount each year.

For example, a $30,000 vehicle depreciating at 15% annually would have an estimated value of $25,500 after one year. In the second year, the 15% depreciation would apply to $25,500 rather than the original $30,000.

Actual depreciation depends on the vehicle's make, model, mileage, age, condition and market demand. This calculator provides an estimate rather than a guaranteed resale value.
