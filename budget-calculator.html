How the Budget Calculator works

This calculator compares your total monthly income with your monthly expenses to estimate how much money you have remaining each month.

Monthly income includes your take-home income plus any other regular income you receive. Expenses include housing, utilities, food, transport, insurance, debt payments, entertainment and other regular spending.

Income spent shows the percentage of your monthly income used to cover expenses. Income remaining shows the percentage left after those expenses have been paid.

If your expenses are higher than your income, the monthly money remaining figure will be negative, showing the estimated monthly shortfall.

Results are estimates for budgeting and planning purposes only.
