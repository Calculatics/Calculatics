How the Rent vs Buy Calculator works

This calculator compares estimated housing costs over the comparison period you choose.

Buying costs include the down payment, mortgage interest, property tax, home insurance and estimated maintenance. Mortgage principal payments are treated as equity rather than a housing cost.

The estimated value of the home is calculated using the annual home appreciation rate entered. Estimated home equity includes the projected home value minus the remaining mortgage balance.

Renting costs are calculated using the starting monthly rent and the annual rent increase entered.

This is a simplified comparison for planning purposes. It does not include every possible cost or financial factor, such as closing costs, selling costs, HOA fees, mortgage insurance, investment returns, tax deductions or changes in property tax and insurance.
