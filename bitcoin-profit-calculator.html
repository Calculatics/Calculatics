How does the Bitcoin Profit Calculator work?

This calculator estimates your Bitcoin profit or loss after buying and selling fees. Enter the price you paid per BTC, the price you sell at and your investment amount.

The calculator determines how much Bitcoin you purchased, its value at the selling price and your profit after trading fees.

How to calculate Bitcoin profit

Bitcoin profit is calculated by subtracting your total purchase cost from your net selling proceeds.

Your gross selling value equals the amount of BTC purchased multiplied by the Bitcoin selling price. The selling fee is then deducted.

Bitcoin profit calculation example

Suppose you invest $1,000 in Bitcoin when BTC costs $60,000 and sell when the price reaches $90,000.

You purchase approximately 0.01666667 BTC, which has a gross selling value of $1,500.

With a 0.5% buying fee ($5) and a 0.5% selling fee ($7.50), your total Bitcoin profit is $487.50.

How are Bitcoin trading fees calculated?

The buying fee is charged in addition to your investment amount. For example, a $1,000 investment with a 0.5% buying fee has a total cost of $1,005.

The selling fee is deducted from the gross selling value. The calculator uses these costs to estimate your net profit and return on investment.

What is the Bitcoin break-even price?

The break-even BTC selling price is the Bitcoin price required to recover your total purchase cost after selling fees.

Selling above this price produces a profit, while selling below it produces a loss.

Does this calculator include Bitcoin taxes?

No. The calculator does not include capital gains taxes, network fees, exchange spreads, slippage or currency conversion charges.

Bitcoin prices are volatile, and investment values can rise or fall significantly. Calculations are estimates rather than guarantees of future returns.
