Understanding cryptocurrency investment returns

Cryptocurrency investment performance depends on purchase price, selling price, investment size and transaction costs.

Even relatively small trading fees can affect your overall returns, particularly when making frequent purchases or selling smaller positions.

What is dollar-cost averaging?

Dollar-cost averaging involves investing a fixed amount at regular intervals rather than making a single investment at one point in time.

This approach spreads purchases across different market prices but does not eliminate investment risk or guarantee a profit.

Important cryptocurrency risks

Cryptocurrency prices are highly volatile and can rise or fall significantly. Calculator results are estimates based on your inputs, not predictions or guarantees of future performance.

These tools do not provide financial advice. Consider transaction costs, taxes and your financial circumstances before making decisions.
