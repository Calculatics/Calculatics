How property tax is calculated

This calculator estimates property tax by multiplying the home value you enter by the property tax rate.

For example, a $400,000 home with a 1.10% property tax rate would have an estimated annual property tax of $4,400.

Actual property taxes can differ because local governments may use an assessed value rather than the property's market value. Exemptions, assessment rules, local tax districts and other charges can also affect the amount due.

This calculator provides estimates only. Check with your local tax authority or assessor for the property tax rules and rates that apply to a specific property.
