How the Credit Card Payoff Calculator works

The calculator estimates how long it could take to repay your credit card balance when you make the same payment each month.

Interest is calculated monthly using the APR you enter. Each monthly payment first covers the interest charged, with the remaining amount reducing the outstanding balance.

If your monthly payment is too low to cover the interest being charged, the calculator will warn that the balance will not be paid off at that payment amount.

This is a simplified estimate for planning purposes. Actual credit card interest can vary because of daily interest calculations, fees, purchases, promotional rates and changes to your APR.
