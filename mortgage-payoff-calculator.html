How the mortgage payoff calculator works

This calculator estimates the monthly principal and interest payment required to pay off your remaining mortgage balance within your chosen payoff term.

It compares that payment with the estimated payment required over your current remaining mortgage term and shows the additional monthly payment, time saved and estimated interest savings.

The calculation assumes a fixed interest rate and regular monthly payments. It does not include property taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA fees or other housing costs.

This calculator provides estimates for planning purposes only. Your lender's payoff figures may differ.
