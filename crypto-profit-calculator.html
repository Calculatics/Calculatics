How the Crypto Profit Calculator works

This free calculator estimates how much money you make or lose when buying and selling cryptocurrency, including percentage-based trading fees.

Enter the price per coin when purchased, the price when sold, the amount invested and the fees charged by your cryptocurrency exchange.

How to calculate crypto profit

Cryptocurrency profit or loss is calculated by subtracting your total purchase cost from your net selling proceeds.

Your gross selling value is the number of coins purchased multiplied by the selling price. The selling fee is deducted from that amount.

How are cryptocurrency trading fees calculated?

The amount invested is used to buy cryptocurrency before fees. The buying fee is added to your purchase cost, while the selling fee is deducted from your selling proceeds.

Crypto profit calculation example

Suppose you invest $1,000 in cryptocurrency at $50,000 per coin and sell it at $65,000 per coin.

You purchase 0.02 coins, which are worth $1,300 at the selling price.

With a 0.5% buying fee and a 0.5% selling fee, the total profit is $288.50.

What is the break-even crypto price?

Your break-even selling price is the price at which you recover your total purchase cost after selling fees.

Selling above this price produces a profit, while selling below it produces a loss.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. This calculator does not include capital gains taxes, network fees, spreads, slippage or currency conversion charges.

Cryptocurrency markets are volatile. These calculations are estimates and are not predictions of future returns.
