How the mortgage calculator works

The principal and interest payment uses the standard fixed-rate mortgage amortization formula. Property tax and insurance are converted to monthly amounts and added to the payment estimate.

This is an estimate for planning purposes. Actual mortgage costs can also include items such as mortgage insurance, HOA fees, closing costs and lender-specific charges.
