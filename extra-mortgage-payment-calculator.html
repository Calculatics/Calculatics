How extra mortgage payments can help

Making additional payments toward your mortgage principal can reduce the amount of interest charged over the life of the loan and may allow you to pay off your mortgage earlier.

This calculator provides estimates only. Your actual mortgage terms, payment schedule and lender rules may affect the results.

How do extra mortgage payments save interest?

Additional payments applied to mortgage principal reduce the balance on which future interest is calculated. For example, paying an extra $100 per month means $1,200 of additional principal payments over a year, assuming your lender applies them directly to principal. The resulting interest savings depend on the remaining balance, rate and loan term. Check for prepayment limits or penalties.
